AI/ML developer in Lahore, Pakistan. Incoming University of Toronto student (September 2027). On a gap year I am self-studying University of Toronto computer science coursework and following an AI engineering roadmap.

Founder of Digital Inqalab, an AI-driven education technology initiative that has taught more than 100 underserved children. I built **Maths AI Tutor**, an offline Urdu-language tutor, for those students.

**Focus areas:** forecasting models, reliable language-model applications, accessible education software.
